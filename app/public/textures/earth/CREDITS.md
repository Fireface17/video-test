# Earth textures

From the three.js examples (`examples/textures/planets/` in https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js, MIT), derived from
NASA imagery (Blue Marble / Black Marble, public domain), used unchanged:
`earth_day_4096.jpg` (surface), `earth_night_4096.jpg` (city lights at night),
`earth_bump_roughness_clouds_4096.jpg` (R relief, G roughness, B clouds).
