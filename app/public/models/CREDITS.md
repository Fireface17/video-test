# Human models

Rigged bodies for the people of light (`app/src/glow/lib/people.ts`), taken unchanged from the three.js
examples (`examples/models/gltf/` in https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js):

- `Xbot.glb`, `Michelle.glb` — Mixamo characters (Adobe). Mixamo characters and animations are royalty-free
  for use in your own projects, including commercial ones, but not for redistribution as standalone assets.
- `readyplayer.me.glb` — a Ready Player Me avatar.

They are drawn only as glowing silhouettes; their textures are not used.
