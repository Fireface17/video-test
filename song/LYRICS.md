# Текст: YOU SAID FOREVER (полная песня)

Оригинальный текст. Хук — вариант A. Вставлять в Suno целиком, вместе с тегами в квадратных скобках.
Пояснения в тегах `[Тег | ...]` — практика сообщества: если Suno их игнорирует, делаем A/B с голыми тегами.

```
[Intro | soft sad piano motif alone, airy pads, no drums, no vocal]

[Verse 1 | soft airy female vocal, filtered beat, no kick]
cold light on the window
your coat is on the chair
i talk into the silence
like you are standing there

[Pre-Chorus | rising build, snare roll, kick comes back]
i keep the porch light burning
all through the night

[Chorus | jumpstyle drop, hard kick and deep 808 on beat one, piano motif unchanged]
you said forever
then you were gone
you said forever
then you were gone

[Verse 2 | half-time beat, piano motif unchanged, same female vocal]
every song is quieter
every road is long
i wear your name like armor
but i'm not that strong

[Pre-Chorus | rising build, snare roll, kick comes back]
i keep the porch light burning
all through the night

[Chorus | jumpstyle drop, hard kick and deep 808, piano motif unchanged]
you said forever
then you were gone
you said forever
then you were gone

[Bridge | drums cut, piano and voice only, very soft]
if heaven has a window
leave it open wide
i still hear you
in an empty room

[Final Chorus | loudest, full arrangement, piano motif one octave up, same female vocal]
you said forever
then you were gone
you said forever
then you were gone

[Outro | drums cut, last piano hit, voice fades]
then you were gone
[End]
```
