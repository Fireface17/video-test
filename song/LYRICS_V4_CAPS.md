# v4 — капс там, где кричать (теги как в v2)

Капс в Suno часто читается как «громче / на крике» — практика сообщества, не документировано.
Куплеты строчными, крик только в припевах и последнем слове пре-корусов.

```
[Instrumental Intro]
[piano motif alone, no vocal, eight bars]

[Verse 1 | soft airy female vocal, quiet, filtered beat, no kick]
cold light on the window
your coat is on the chair
i talk into the silence
like you are standing there

[Pre-Chorus | rising build, snare roll, kick comes back]
i keep the porch light burning
all through the NIGHT

[Chorus | powerful belted female vocal, emotional, jumpstyle drop, hard kick and deep 808]
YOU SAID FOREVER
THEN YOU WERE GONE
YOU SAID FOREVER
THEN YOU WERE GONE

[Verse 2 | half-time beat, piano motif unchanged, same female vocal]
every song is quieter
every road is long
i wear your name like armor
but i'm not that strong

[Pre-Chorus | rising build, snare roll, kick comes back]
i keep the porch light burning
all through the NIGHT

[Chorus | powerful belted female vocal, emotional, jumpstyle drop, hard kick and deep 808]
YOU SAID FOREVER
THEN YOU WERE GONE
YOU SAID FOREVER
THEN YOU WERE GONE

[Bridge | drums cut, piano and voice only, very soft]
if heaven has a window
leave it open wide
i still hear you
in an empty room

[Final Chorus | most powerful belted female vocal, loudest, piano motif one octave up]
YOU SAID FOREVER
THEN YOU WERE GONE
YOU SAID FOREVER
THEN YOU WERE GOOONE

[Outro | drums cut, last piano hit, voice fades]
then you were gone
[End]
```
